About Equity Signal

Equity market intelligence, quantitative strategies and rule-based signals for Indian equities.

Equity Signal is a research and decision-support tool, not a stock-tip service. Every signal is generated by a defined, testable rule — not a hunch. The platform's job is to notice patterns worth your attention and tell you about them; it does not tell you what to buy or sell.

Some strategies are still under active development and only partially tested.

Investments in the securities market are subject to market risks. Read all related information carefully before making any investment decision.
